Labor Day Wrestling Fundraiser

Wrestlers, coaches, families, and supporters spent part of Labor Day training together and raising money for the Roark and Stephens families. Slate Wrestling Academy and The Colosseum Training Center shared the mat for one purpose: helping two families their wrestling community cares deeply about.
