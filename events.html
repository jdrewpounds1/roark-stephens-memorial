Labor Day Wrestling Fundraiser

Kids, coaches, families, and supporters gave part of their Labor Day holiday to train together and raise money for the Roark and Stephens families. Slate Wrestling Academy and The Colosseum Training Center came together on one mat for one purpose: supporting two families their wrestling community cares deeply about.

Approximately $3,400 raised. The day brought wrestlers, coaches, parents, friends, and community supporters together for training, fellowship, and a cause bigger than wrestling.
