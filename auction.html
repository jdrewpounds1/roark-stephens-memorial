Thank You for Showing Up for These Families.

The Roark & Stephens Strong Silent Auction is officially closed. We are deeply grateful to the businesses that donated items and experiences, and to every person who visited, shared, submitted a bid, and supported the Roark and Stephens families.

Community partners

Thank You to Our Auction Sponsors

These businesses stepped forward with more than $1,200 in donated training, recovery, photography and wrestling experiences. Their generosity gave this fundraiser something real to offer and helped keep the focus where it belongs: supporting the families.

Goldmine PerformanceDonated four private assisted stretch sessions and three private performance-training sessions. Cela PhotographyDonated a professional athlete photo shoot in Dallas, Georgia. Slate Wrestling AcademyDonated a one-month wrestling training membership. The Colosseum Training CenterDonated a one-month wrestling training membership.
