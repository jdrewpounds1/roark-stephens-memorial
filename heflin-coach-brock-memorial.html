Let’s Make the Trip and Fill Those Stands.

The Roark family may be newer to Cleburne County, but with children on all three PARD teams—and Brock helping coach the Bronze team—they quickly became part of Heflin’s football family.

Now is our chance in West Georgia to return that support. Come early, stand with the family for the memorial prayer, stay for the football, and let the Roark children see two communities wrapped around them.

  • Arrive before 9:00 AM so you are in place for the prayer.
  • Wear hunter green or camo in Coach Brock’s honor.
  • Bring your family, make some noise, and support Bronze, Silver and Gold.
  • Share this page with wrestling clubs, football families, schools and friends across West Georgia.

One Morning. One Big Show of Love.

We cannot take away what this family has endured. We can make sure they never have to wonder whether their communities are standing beside them.
